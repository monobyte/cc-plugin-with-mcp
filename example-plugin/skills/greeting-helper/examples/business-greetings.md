# Business Greeting Examples

Practical examples of professional greetings for various business scenarios.

## Client Onboarding Email

```markdown
Good morning, Sarah.

It is a pleasure to welcome you as our newest client. We are honored that
you have chosen to partner with us for your digital transformation journey.

Your dedicated account team is already preparing for our kickoff meeting
scheduled for next Tuesday. In the meantime, please find attached our
welcome package with all the relevant documentation.

Should you have any questions before our meeting, please do not hesitate
to reach out.

Warm regards,
Michael Thompson
Senior Account Manager
```

## Partner Introduction

```markdown
Dear Dr. Chen,

It is a pleasure to make your acquaintance. I am reaching out on behalf
of Acme Corporation regarding a potential collaboration opportunity that
I believe would be mutually beneficial.

Our team has been following your research in sustainable materials, and
we see significant synergies with our current innovation initiatives.

Would you be available for a brief call next week to explore this further?

Best regards,
Jennifer Martinez
Director of Strategic Partnerships
```

## Vendor Communication

```markdown
Good afternoon, Mr. Rodriguez.

Thank you for submitting your proposal for the infrastructure upgrade
project. Our technical team has reviewed the documentation and would
like to schedule a follow-up discussion to address a few clarifications.

Please let us know your availability for a 45-minute call this week
or early next week.

We appreciate your prompt attention to this matter.

Sincerely,
David Park
Procurement Manager
```

## Board Meeting Opening

```markdown
Good morning, distinguished board members.

It is my privilege to welcome you to our Q4 quarterly review. We have
prepared a comprehensive agenda covering our financial performance,
strategic initiatives, and outlook for the coming fiscal year.

Before we proceed, I would like to acknowledge the exceptional efforts
of our teams across all divisions during this challenging quarter.

Let us begin with the financial summary.
```

Chief Executive Officer

## Using MCP Tools for Business Greetings

### Generate a formal greeting programmatically:

```
Use the greet tool:
- name: "Dr. Williams"
- style: "formal"

Result: "Good day, Dr. Williams. It is a pleasure to make your acquaintance."
```

### Combine with timestamp for context:

```
1. Use get_timestamp (format: "readable")
   Result: "Monday, January 15, 2024, 9:30 AM EST"

2. Use greet tool (name: "Ms. Johnson", style: "formal")
   Result: "Good day, Ms. Johnson. It is a pleasure to make your acquaintance."

3. Combine for morning business email opening:
   "Good morning, Ms. Johnson. Thank you for taking the time to connect
   with us today, January 15th."
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Always personalize**: Use the recipient's name and correct title
2. **Match the occasion**: Adjust formality based on the business context
3. **Be concise**: Business greetings should be respectful but efficient
4. **Include purpose**: Hint at the message's intent in the greeting
5. **Close appropriately**: Match the closing salutation to the opening tone
